# 🍕 Pizza Sales Analysis using SQL & Excel

## 📊 Project Snapshot

**Domain:** Food & Beverage Analytics

**Objective:** Analyze pizza sales data to identify revenue drivers, customer ordering patterns, product performance, and sales trends to support data-driven business decisions.

**Tools Used:**
- SQL
- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- Pivot Charts
- Slicers

**Techniques:**
- KPI Analysis
- Sales Trend Analysis
- Product Performance Analysis
- Dashboard Development
- Business Insight Generation

**Outcome:**
Developed an interactive sales dashboard and used SQL to analyze revenue, customer ordering patterns, product performance, and operational trends for business decision-making.

---

## 📌 Overview

Pizza sales analysis enables businesses to understand customer purchasing behavior, identify high-performing products, monitor revenue trends, and optimize operations. By combining SQL-based data analysis with an interactive Excel dashboard, this project transforms raw transactional data into meaningful business insights.

The analysis focuses on key sales metrics, product performance, sales trends, and customer ordering behavior to support data-driven decisions for inventory planning, marketing strategies, and overall business growth.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

- Peak order volumes occur during lunch and evening hours.
- Classic pizzas contribute the highest share of revenue.
- Large-sized pizzas generate the highest sales revenue.
- Customer demand varies across different pizza categories.
- A few pizza varieties contribute significantly more sales than others.
- Several pizzas consistently underperform and may require promotional strategies.

---

## 📋 Business Recommendations

- Increase inventory for top-selling pizzas during peak hours.
- Promote low-selling pizzas through discounts or combo offers.
- Optimize staffing during busy periods.
- Focus marketing campaigns on high-performing pizza categories.
- Review menu offerings for consistently underperforming pizzas.
